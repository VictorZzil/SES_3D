# Evidence and upstream materials

The evidence directory is derived from the authors' benchmark evaluations. It includes the saved numeric results, benchmark object identifiers, and subset manifests needed by the replay scripts. The papers and original project pages cited in the README are the sources for the data, methods, and dependencies used to produce those records.

COSSAD's upstream repository identifies its license as [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). The COSSAD source and encoder checkpoints are upstream dependencies. Users obtain them from COSSAD and follow its licensing terms. The local scoring entry point implements the SESAD equations from aligned descriptors and does not require COSSAD code to run.

The official Real3D-AD and Anomaly-ShapeNet project pages identify their dataset licenses as CC BY 4.0. The evaluation records here contain derived scores and benchmark identifiers. Obtain benchmark clouds from the respective providers.

This is a submission-review copy. Public redistribution of the authors' code and derived evidence follows the license and permissions approved by their rights holders.
